# Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 — Official Competition Facts

Researched Oct 4, 2026. Sources: the official announcement (https://developers.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-vr-start-developer-competition/), the VR Start program page (https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/programs/start/), and the competition hub + official rules (https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/).

## Organizer
Meta Platforms, Inc. Announced at Meta Connect 2026. Judged in two stages by Meta + third-party judges (AI tools may assist; final decisions are human).

## Key dates
- **Submission window:** Sept 24, 2026 – **Nov 18, 2026, 8:00 PM UTC**
- Judging: on or around Nov 18 – Dec 9, 2026
- **Winners announced:** on or around Dec 11, 2026
- No submission changes allowed after the deadline

## Tracks
1. **Entertainment** — lean-back spatial media: spatial cinema, interactive video, music visualization, immersive storytelling, spatial audio, media companion apps
2. **Gaming** — hands-first (required) or eyes-and-hands; puzzle, strategy, casual, social, narrative — genres that thrive seated, without controllers
3. **Productivity** — multi-panel workspaces, task management, creative tools, habits tied to recurring contexts (morning routine, commute, wind-down)

## Divisions
- **New Experience** — conceived and built within the competition window (from Sept 24, 2026). No pre-existing codebases, no shipped titles, no repurposed early-access builds.
- **Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience** — pre-existing project with a meaningful new feature/mode/platform integration shipped during the window (e.g., adding hand interactions, multiplayer, an MR mode). Bug fixes and cosmetic changes do not qualify.

Limit: one prize per entry.

## Judging criteria (equally weighted)
1. **Innovation & Creativity** — originality, creative ambition, relevance to the chosen track; uniquely leverages Meta device capabilities (spatial depth, hands-first design) to drive repeat usage
2. **Experience Design** — design, mechanics, user journey for the intended audience; seated, hands-first emphasis; intuitive navigation, clear onboarding, habit-forming purpose; passthrough must be purposeful (the real room meaningfully changes the experience); FoV-aware design
3. **Technical Implementation** — technical expertise, reliability, strategic use of platform tools: gaze interactions (ISDK 77+), hand tracking, passthrough, spatial anchoring; **minimum 60fps on Quest hardware**; solid and bug-free
4. **Polish & Presentation** — UI/UX, art direction, sound design, submission materials; video must show **real gameplay** — the rules explicitly warn against leaning on AI-generated video to carry the pitch

## Hard requirements (non-negotiable)
- **Hands-first:** the entire experience must be completable with bare hands, end to end. Controller support is welcome but optional. The test: can someone finish it without ever pairing a controller?
- **Policy compliance:** Meta VR Start Program Terms, Community Standards, Code of Conduct in VR, Developer App/Content Policies
- **Seated-optimized:** stationary use, no large physical movement ("airplane seat test" — every interaction works in a 2-foot radius)
- **Fast enter/exit:** quick cold start, clean pause/resume, satisfying within 10 minutes
- **Original:** core experience entrant-built. Thin wrappers around existing platforms/services are penalized ("take it away" test — remove the third-party service; is there still a project?)
- **Tech baseline:** runs on Quest 3/3S/2/Pro, Horizon OS v85+

## Submission requirements
- Register on Devpost ("Join Hackathon"); entrant must be a **Meta VR Start Program member** by submission time (requires Meta developer sign-in — currently blocked, awaiting Chris)
- Native builds (Unity/Unreal/Godot): APK uploaded to the Meta VR Developer Dashboard under a release channel named "Competition"
- WebXR (IWSDK): hosted link for judges (GitHub Pages, Vercel, etc.)
- Demo video under 3 minutes, real device/emulator footage, public on YouTube or Vimeo
- Text description: inspiration, how it was built, future plans, target launch date
- Track + Division selection

## Eligibility
- 18+, not resident in Brazil, Quebec, or sanctioned regions
- Active Meta account with Developer Access
- One entry per individual (solo, or as one team's representative)
- Project must not have prior Meta development funding
