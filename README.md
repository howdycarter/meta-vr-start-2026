# Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 — Entry Workspace

## Objective
Decide GO/NO-GO on entering the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 ($1M, 20 prizes), and if GO, ship a hands-first Quest productivity experience that scores on all four equally-weighted judging criteria.

## Source of Truth
- Competition rules: `docs/competition.md` (researched from official Meta + Devpost pages, Oct 4 2026)
- Win plan + honest feasibility assessment: `docs/win-plan.md`
- Announcement: https://developers.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-vr-start-developer-competition/
- Program: https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/programs/start/
- Competition hub + official rules: https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/

## Done When
- [ ] GO/NO-GO decision made (see gates below)
- [ ] If GO: working hands-first app on Quest (60fps), submitted via Devpost before **Nov 18, 2026, 8:00 PM UTC**
- [ ] Demo video under 3 minutes showing REAL gameplay on device (AI-generated video is called out in the rules as a negative signal)
- [ ] Chris explicitly approves the submission before anything is posted

## Acceptance Criteria
- Fully usable with hands end-to-end — a judge must complete the entire experience without ever pairing a controller
- Seated-optimized; fast cold start; satisfying within 10 minutes
- Runs on Quest 3/3S/2/Pro, Horizon OS v85+, minimum 60fps
- Original work; core experience entrant-built (thin wrappers around existing services are penalized)

## Constraints
- **Gate 1 — enrollment:** requires Meta developer sign-in + VR Start program membership by submission time. Currently blocked on Meta identity selection, awaiting Chris.
- **Gate 2 — hardware:** no Quest device in hand; judges test on device. XR Simulator exists but is not a substitute for on-device hand-tracking iteration.
- **Gate 3 — skill gap:** no XR background. Hand-tracking UX, spatial design, and Quest performance budgets are specialty skills.
- One entry per individual (as solo entrant or as one team's representative)
- No changes allowed after the submission deadline; judging is on the entry as submitted

## Non-Goals
- Chasing the $100k top prize as the plan's premise — the plan targets a credible, complete entry first
- Controller-based gameplay of any kind
- Anything that violates Meta's content policies or the "take it away" originality test

## Verifier
Chris clears the three gates (or kills the entry), reviews on-device gameplay, then approves the Devpost submission.
